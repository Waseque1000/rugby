# The Road to LA 2028: Home Soil Advantage

In 2028, the Olympic Games will return to American soil in Los Angeles, California. For USA Rugby Sevens, this milestone marks the biggest commercial and competitive opportunity in the history of the sport in North America.

## Building the Next Generation

The journey through Rio 2016, Tokyo 2020, and Paris 2024 forged international credibility. Now, younger emerging stars like Aaron Cummings, Sammy Sullivan, Autumn Czaplicki, and Sarah Levy are stepping forward as the vanguard of the squad.

## The Role of Collegiate and Club Ecosystems

From the Chicago Lions to the Denver Barbarians, and collegiate programs at Dartmouth, Cal Berkeley, Army West Point, and Wisconsin, the player pipeline has never been wider or more robust. As the countdown to 2028 ticks down, every SVNS tournament serves as vital preparation for the biggest stage in sports.
