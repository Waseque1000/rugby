# The Golden Era of USA Women's Rugby Sevens

Over the past decade, international rugby sevens has undergone a seismic shift. The sport has evolved from a niche Olympic newcomer into an arena-filling global spectacle. At the absolute center of this transformation stands the **USA Women's Eagles Sevens**.

Blending raw athletic dynamism with collegiate discipline and unapologetic joy, this squad has not only captured international podium finishes—they have captured the cultural imagination of the sporting world.

## The Triad of Power: Kelter, Kirshe, and Sullivan

Every great team possesses a distinct identity. For the United States, that identity is built on relentless collision dominance and lightning-fast transition defense.

### 1. Alev Kelter: The Ultimate Hybrid Competitor
Few athletes across any discipline possess a resume comparable to Alev Kelter. A collegiate ice hockey standout and soccer star at the University of Wisconsin, Kelter brought ice-hockey toughness, kicking accuracy, and devastating tackle technique into international rugby. As a three-time Olympian (Rio 2016, Tokyo 2020, and Paris 2024), Kelter serves as the heartbeat and tactical compass of the squad.

### 2. Kristi Kirshe: The Soccer Crossover Sensation
Kirshe's pathway from NCAA Division III soccer at Williams College directly into the World Rugby Dream Team is now modern rugby lore. Her ability to execute sharp 90-degree cuts at top sprint speed creates defensive overloads out of thin air.

### 3. Sammy Sullivan: Military Precision Meets High-Velocity Rugby
An Army officer developed through Army West Point and the US Army World Class Athlete Program (WCAP), Sammy Sullivan embodies pure forward power. Whether securing restarts in aerial duels or defending the fringe channel, Sullivan sets the physical tone in the opening 60 seconds of every match.

## The Tactical Blueprint: Winning the Restart Battle

In Rugby Sevens, possession is everything. When teams concede a try, they immediately restart with a drop kick. If you can recover 60% of your own restarts, you starve opponents of the ball.

The Eagles have engineered the most lethal restart scheme on the SVNS circuit:
- **Pinpoint Hang Time**: Drop kicks targeted with 3.8+ seconds of hang time to the 10-meter line.
- **Dual Towers**: Positioning tall aerial threats alongside mobile sweepers to win second-phase knock-downs.
- **Immediate Wide Release**: Bypassing breakdown rucks instantly to exploit the unstructured edge defense.

## Looking Forward to the 2028 Horizon

With the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games taking place on American soil, the momentum behind this squad is unprecedented. As new collegiate talents join the Chula Vista residency, the standard set by Kelter, Kirshe, and Sullivan ensures the future of American rugby has never been brighter.
