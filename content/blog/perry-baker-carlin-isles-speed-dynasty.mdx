# The Speed Dynasty: Redefining the Sevens Wing

For nearly a decade, opposing teams on the World Rugby Sevens Series shared a single, terrifying tactical concern: do not give Perry Baker or Carlin Isles an inch of open prairie.

## Perry Baker: The Magician from Daytona Beach

Perry Baker wasn't merely fast; he possessed arguably the most deceptive acceleration and balanced swerve in rugby history. Twice awarded World Rugby Men's Sevens Player of the Year (2017 and 2018), Baker accumulated more than 290 career series tries, breaking ankles and game plans alike.

His ability to read defensive spacing, chip and chase, and recover his own kicks made him a complete three-dimensional threat on both sides of the ball.

## Carlin Isles: The Fastest Man in Rugby

Hailing from Massillon, Ohio, Carlin Isles arrived in rugby with a 10.13-second 100-meter track pedigree. What initially looked like an intriguing athletic crossover rapidly turned into a clinical finishing showcase. Opposing sweepers had to adjust their defensive lines by 15 meters simply to account for his straight-line velocity.

## Tactical Impact on Modern Sevens

Together with cerebral playmakers like Madison Hughes, Danny Barrett, and Folau Niua, the Baker-Isles era proved that American athletic crossover potential could dominate Tier-1 rugby nations when matched with world-class skill development.
