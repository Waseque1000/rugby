# The Complete 2026 World Rugby SVNS Guide

World Rugby's flagship annual competition, **HSBC SVNS**, features the top 12 men's and top 12 women's national teams competing across iconic global cities. Here is your definitive roadmap to the season.

## How the SVNS Championship Format Works

The SVNS model emphasizes high-stakes drama from the opening kickoff in December through the Grand Final in June:

1. **Regular Season Stops**: Teams earn series standings points across 7 worldwide destination tournaments (Dubai, Cape Town, Perth, Vancouver, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Los Angeles).
2. **The Top Eight Cutoff**: Following the regular season, the top 8 teams in the standings qualify for the **Championship Grand Final**.
3. **The Play-Off Drama**: Teams ranked 9th through 12th must compete in a relegation playoff tournament against the top four teams from the World Rugby Challenger Series.

## Where to Watch USA Matches

In the United States, all SVNS matches are streamed live and on-demand via **Peacock**, with select weekend finals broadcast across **CNBC** and **NBC Sports**.

International audiences can follow real-time match streams via **RugbyPass TV**.
