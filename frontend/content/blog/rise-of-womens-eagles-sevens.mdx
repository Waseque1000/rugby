# The Rise of the USA Women's Eagles Sevens

When rugby sevens made its Olympic debut at Rio 2016, the American squad arrived with fierce determination and an undeniable point to prove. Led by legends such as Jillion Potter, Kelly Griffin, and a young dual-sport phenomenon named Alev Kelter, the Eagles laid the groundwork for an era of sustainable high performance.

## A Legacy of Grit and Resiliency

The early years of the World Rugby Women's Sevens Series required players to balance full-time careers, military commitments, and collegiate studies with international tours. Athletes like Sammy Sullivan—who honed her craft through Army West Point and the US Army World Class Athlete Program (WCAP)—exemplify the dual-career discipline that came to define USA Women's Rugby.

> "Every single jersey handoff represents not just a weekend tournament, but years of grassroots dedication across American clubs and colleges."

## The Modern SVNS Dynasty

Over the 2024 and 2026 World Rugby SVNS cycles, the Women's Eagles evolved from underdog contenders into fixture finalists. With the visionary leadership of players like Kristi Kirshe, Lauren Doyle, and explosive finishers like Sarah Levy, the United States established itself in the top tier of international rugby.

### Key Factors Behind the Program's Success:
- **Collegiate Rugby Integration**: Programs like Dartmouth, Penn State, Harvard, and Wisconsin feed world-class talent into the national residency.
- **Athletic Crossover Programs**: Identifying elite track, soccer, and ice hockey competitors with spatial intelligence and top-end speed.
- **Professionalized Training Environments**: State-of-the-art conditioning and video analysis at the Chula Vista Elite Athlete Training Center.

As the team sets its sights on future international competitions and the road to the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games, the foundation built by these athletes promises an enduring legacy for North American rugby.
