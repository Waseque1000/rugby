# Inside the American Collegiate Rugby Pipeline

Unlike traditional rugby heartlands in New Zealand, South Africa, or England where professional club academies sign teenage players, the United States relies on a distinctive domestic engine: **college rugby**.

From Ivy League powerhouses to military academies and Big Ten programs, university rugby clubs and varsity programs provide the crucible where raw athleticism transforms into international caliber skill.

## 1. University of California, Berkeley (Cal)
Cal Rugby represents one of the most storied traditions in American intercollegiate athletics. Under legendary coaching, Cal has captured over thirty national collegiate championships and supplied national sevens stars such as **Danny Barrett** and **Zack Test**.

## 2. Dartmouth College
The Big Green developed one of the sharpest rugby intellects in American sevens history: **Madison Hughes**. Hughes served as USA Sevens captain while still a college undergraduate, demonstrating how collegiate rugby can forge elite international captains.

## 3. Army West Point
West Point's women's varsity program has set the standard for high-performance collegiate development. Athletes like **Sammy Sullivan** leveraged the academy's world-class athletic facilities and leadership culture to step seamlessly into the World Rugby Dream Team.

## 4. University of Wisconsin
Wisconsin provided the launching pad for **Alev Kelter**. The athletic crossover culture at Big Ten schools—where athletes frequently compete at elite levels across multiple sports—creates the exceptional hand-eye coordination and spatial instinct needed for modern sevens.

## 5. Brigham Young University (BYU) & Utah Pathway
Utah has emerged as a premier rugby hotbed in the United States. BYU has produced explosive sevens backs like **Maka Unufe**, known for his sublime step and instinctive support running.
